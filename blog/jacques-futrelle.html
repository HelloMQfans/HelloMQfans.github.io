Who was Jacques Futrelle?

The creator of Professor Van Dusen

Jacques Heath Futrelle (April 9, 1875 – April 15, 1912) was an American journalist and mystery writer.

Jacques Futrelle

Futrelle is best known for writing short detective stories featuring Professor Augustus S. F. X. Van Dusen, also known as "The Thinking Machine" for his use of logic.

Futrelle during his lifetime

Futrelle was famous during his lifetime.

  • His eccentric, logic-driven detective character, Professor Augustus S. F. X. Van Dusen (known as "The Thinking Machine"), made him a household name and a pioneer of early American detective fiction.
  • By the early 20th century, Futrelle was a well-known and respected novelist and short-story writer, widely read in both the United States and abroad.
  • The press frequently referred to him by prestigious titles like the "American Conan Doyle" as his stories gained immense popularity in newspapers and magazines.

The Titanic

Futrelle died in the sinking of the RMS Titanic in April 1912.

Additional information

  • Futrelle was inspired by the detective works of Sherlock Holmes, first published in 1887, by Arthur Conan Doyle.
  • Doyle and Futrelle did not meet personally. It is not known if they had correspondence.
  • The character of the reporter Hutchinson Hatch, assistant of Professor Van Dusen, is roughly equivalent to the role of Dr. Watson in the stories of Sherlock Holmes.
  • In the original works from Futrelle, the role of Hatch is very secondary. But because I like this character, the adaptations have a more prominent role for him.

The German Radio Plays

Between 1978 and 1999 the German radio station RIAS produced and broadcast 79 radio plays based on the characters of Van Dusen and Hatch.

A few of them were based on original stories by Futrelle, but most of the scripts were new creations by German author Michael Koser.

The role of Hutchinson Hatch is a lot more prominent in the radio plays than it was in the original; Hatch was made into the fictional narrator in the radio version.

The series was revived in 2015 with 37 new cases available via CD and downloads.

Since 2010, the old radio plays have been remastered and made available on CD with comments by Koser, director Rainer Clute, and former cast members.
