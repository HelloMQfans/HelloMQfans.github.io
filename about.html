About The Narrating Mime

The Project

The Narrating Mime is a project dedicated to bringing classic public-domain stories to listeners and readers through narrated recordings.

The collection focuses especially on stories of mystery, science fiction, adventure, and other works from earlier periods of literature.

The Narrations

Each narration has its own page where visitors can find the narrated recording, information about the story and author, and links to related reading materials.

The goal is to make these stories easy to discover, listen to, and explore.

Source Texts

When available, source texts are provided for readers who would like to read along with a narration or explore the original work independently.

Selected materials are available for online reading as well as TXT and PDF downloads.

English and Spanish

The project includes English source materials and Spanish translations for selected works.

The Spanish materials are intended to make these classic stories accessible to a wider audience of readers and listeners.

Public Domain Works

The Narrating Mime focuses on works that are believed to be in the public domain. Source and publication information is considered when preparing materials for the site.

Public-domain status can vary by country and by particular edition or translation. Visitors should consider the applicable copyright laws in their own jurisdiction.
